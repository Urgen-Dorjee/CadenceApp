# Code of conduct

Cadence follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, and welcome people of every background and
level of experience. Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't accepted in issues,
pull requests or discussions.

To report unacceptable behaviour, use
[a private report](https://github.com/Urgen-Dorjee/CadenceApp/security/advisories/new) or
contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.
