Split YouTube jukeboxes into songs. Save whole videos.

Cadence is a free YouTube downloader for your music and your courses. Paste a two-hour jukebox or a movie album: it finds where every song starts, cuts in the gap between songs, then tags and files each one with its cover art.

Download for Windows macOS Linux

No account, no ads, no tracking. MIT license. Source on GitHub.

Jukeboxes become a tagged music library

Most YouTube downloaders give you one long file. Cadence gives you the songs.

  • Finds the songs from chapters, the description or comments, or by listening for the gaps.
  • Cuts in the real silence between songs, so nothing is clipped and nothing spills over.
  • Names songs by their sound (AcoustID and MusicBrainz) when the video doesn't list them.
  • MP3, M4A, Opus, FLAC or the original audio, with album, year, track numbers, cover art and lyrics.
  • Files singer collections under Artists and movie albums under Albums, with a playlist for each.

Whole videos, playlists and courses

Switch to Video to keep lectures, documentaries and talks.

  • Choose the quality (up to 4K) and see the file size before downloading. MP4 plays everywhere; MKV keeps everything.
  • Subtitles in your languages, inside the video or as .srt files, plus chapters and the thumbnail.
  • Courses download in order into their own folder, skipping videos you already have.
  • A built-in player that remembers where you stopped and plays a course in order.

How it works

Three steps, and Cadence remembers what you saved: paste the same link again and it carries on where you left off.

  1. Paste a linkA video, a playlist, or a file from your computer.
  2. Check the songsListen to each cut, drag it if needed, and rename anything. Unsure cuts are marked "Check".
  3. SaveSongs are tagged, given cover art and filed into your library.

Your library, organised

Browse by album, singer or song, with a player, queue, lyrics and a sleep timer. Edit tags in place.

Private by design

Everything runs on your computer. Only what each feature needs leaves it, such as a sound fingerprint for naming songs.

Safe with your data

Daily backups of your history and settings, and every installer is built in public from the source on GitHub.

Questions

Is Cadence really free?

Yes. It's open source under the MIT license, with no ads, accounts or paid tiers.

What makes it different from other YouTube downloaders?

It splits long videos made of many songs (jukeboxes, movie albums, singer collections) into separate, named, tagged songs, and files them into a music library. It also saves whole videos and courses with subtitles and chapters, and plays them.

Does it need my YouTube account?

No. It's only needed for age-restricted videos, or if YouTube asks you to confirm you're not a bot. Then Cadence can use the sign-in of a browser you're already signed in to.

Can it split files I already have?

Yes. Open an audio or video file from your computer and Cadence splits it the same way. Your file is never changed.

Is downloading from YouTube allowed?

Only download videos you own or have permission to download, and respect YouTube's terms and the creators' rights.
